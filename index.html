Emergency? Activate rescue now. Never wait to finish this form. Text recognition is unreliable and cannot exclude danger.

REQUIRED red-flag checklist — every item before disposition

Enter measured blood pressure and tap Yes, No or Unsure for every other item. Unsure counts as Yes except for BP: missing BP means obtain BP now and holds disposition. A blank is not No. Use No only when assessed absent or clinician-confirmed not applicable, never for missing information. Positive alerts appear immediately; completing this checklist must never delay rescue. Ask privately when safe.

Measured blood pressure / Presión arterial medida

SBP ≥160 OR DBP ≥110: immediate clinician assessment, with or without symptoms. Missing/invalid BP never releases disposition; do not delay rescue for measurement.

Measured HR and SpO2 / FC y SpO2 medidas

Stable = HR <110, SBP ≥90 and <160, DBP <110, SpO2 ≥95%, alert and oriented. HR ≥110 or SpO2 <95% (CMQCC) is immediate. Missing/invalid HR or SpO2 holds disposition; do not delay rescue for measurement.

Patient language and interpreter / Idioma e intérprete

Read first by the interpreter rule. Non-English preference without a professional interpreter (family interpreting never counts): trauma/IPV, passive SI and stable PUL cannot be downgraded to URGENT. Free text can only add risk.
